Privacy Policy

Scoop Runner is a single-player arcade game. Last updated September 5, 2026.

What we store on your device

High score, career scoops, mute preference, byline name, and (if you check Record my game) the last run video. These stay on your phone via local storage and IndexedDB. We cannot read them.

What leaves the device

If you file a score to the global desk, we send your chosen name, score, scoop count, and an inferred country code (from your timezone). No account, email, or precise location is collected. Country flags are loaded from a public flag CDN.

What we do not collect

No advertising ID, no analytics SDK, no crash reporter, no contacts, no photos, no microphone. Recording captures only the game canvas, not the camera.

Children

The game is cartoon action suitable for ages 9+. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

Questions: open an issue on the public repository at github.com/sashikanthmeduri/scoop-runner.
